# Kids Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Embedded within families across all city types.
- **Occupations:** Students.
- **Traits:** Curious, highly influenced by peers and media, limited financial autonomy but high influence on household spending.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Rapid adoption of new trends, spread information quickly within their peer networks, vulnerable to external shocks but adaptable.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Gamer:** Highly connected online, introverted in person.
2. **The Athlete:** Social, competitive, active in local sports leagues.
3. **The Scholar:** Focused on academics, smaller but deep social circle.
