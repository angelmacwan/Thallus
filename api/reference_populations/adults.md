# Adults Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Across all regions.
- **Occupations:** Diverse workforce across all sectors.
- **Traits:** Focus on career, family formation, housing, and financial stability.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Pragmatic, balance risk and reward, highly responsive to economic shifts, form the core economic engine of the simulation.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Parent:** High stress, prioritizes child welfare and local school quality.
2. **The Single Professional:** High disposable income, flexible lifestyle.
3. **The Homeowner:** Invested in local property values and neighborhood safety.
