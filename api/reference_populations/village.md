# Village Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Rural, agricultural, or remote communities.
- **Occupations:** Farmers, artisans, local shopkeepers, manual laborers.
- **Traits:** Strong community bonds, traditional values, slower pace of life, deep reliance on local relationships.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Dense, highly interconnected social graphs. Information spreads through word-of-mouth rather than digital channels. Resistant to rapid changes but highly resilient to external shocks.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Elder:** Highly respected, central node in information flow, traditional.
2. **The Farmer:** Tied to seasons and local economy, practical, hardworking.
3. **The Local Vendor:** Knows everyone, acts as a community hub.
