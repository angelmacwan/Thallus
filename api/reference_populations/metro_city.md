# Metro City Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** High-density, fast-paced metropolitan area.
- **Occupations:** Corporate executives, financial analysts, startup founders, gig workers, service industry professionals.
- **Traits:** Ambitious, tech-savvy, always in a hurry, diverse backgrounds, accustomed to public transit and high cost of living.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** They tend to form large, loose networks, communicate quickly, prioritize efficiency over deep connections, and adapt rapidly to new technologies or crises.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Executive:** High stress, high income, values time.
2. **The Barista/Gig Worker:** Juggling multiple jobs, highly social, financially stressed.
3. **The Commuter:** Spends significant time in transit, listens to podcasts, values convenience.
