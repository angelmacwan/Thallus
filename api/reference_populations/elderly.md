# Elderly Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Often concentrated in quieter neighborhoods or retirement communities.
- **Occupations:** Retirees, part-time consultants, volunteers.
- **Traits:** Fixed incomes, focus on healthcare and security, strong local community ties.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Slow to adopt new technologies, resistant to change, highly vulnerable to inflation or healthcare crises, provide stability and institutional memory.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Grandparent:** Focuses on family, provides childcare support.
2. **The Active Retiree:** Volunteers, highly engaged in local politics.
3. **The Isolated Senior:** Vulnerable, relies heavily on social safety nets.
