# Tech Workers Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Distributed globally or clustered in tech hubs.
- **Occupations:** Software engineers, product managers, designers, IT support.
- **Traits:** Highly analytical, early adopters of technology, skeptical of traditional media, remote-work friendly.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Extremely fast information processing through niche digital channels (Discord, Reddit, Twitter). Prone to forming echo chambers. High mobility and disposable income.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Senior Dev:** Opinionated on tech stacks, high income, works irregular hours.
2. **The Startup Founder:** High stress, networking constantly, risk-tolerant.
3. **The Remote Worker:** Geographically isolated but digitally hyper-connected.
