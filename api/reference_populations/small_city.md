# Small City Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Smaller urban areas, often centered around a single industry or college.
- **Occupations:** Services, local industry, healthcare.
- **Traits:** Tight-knit communities, resistant to rapid change, high trust in local institutions.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Information flows through central community hubs (churches, schools, local diners). High solidarity in crises.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Teacher:** Trusted community figure, shapes local opinions.
2. **The Mechanic:** Essential service provider, pragmatic.
3. **The Retiree:** Active in local politics and community boards.
