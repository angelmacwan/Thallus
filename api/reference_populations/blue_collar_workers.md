# Blue Collar Workers Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Industrial zones, construction sites, logistics hubs.
- **Occupations:** Construction, plumbing, electrical, logistics, warehouse.
- **Traits:** Practical, physical, value tangible results, strong union or fraternal affiliations.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Pragmatic decision making. Less influenced by abstract ideological debates, highly influenced by material conditions and local economy.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Foreman:** Leader, responsible, focused on safety and deadlines.
2. **The Apprentice:** Eager, learning the trade, financially constrained.
3. **The Truck Driver:** Isolated at times, connects different geographical zones.
