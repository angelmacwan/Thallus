# Tier 1 City Population Profile

## Demographics
- **Setting:** Large, developed urban centers with strong infrastructure.
- **Occupations:** Professionals, mid-level management, healthcare workers, educators.
- **Traits:** Educated, heavily reliant on digital services, socially active, diverse interests.
- **Behavior in Simulation:** Networked through professional and social clubs, engage heavily in local economy, moderate stress levels compared to Metro City, responsive to local governance changes.

## Typical Agents
1. **The Professional:** Stable income, family-oriented, active in community.
2. **The Student:** Highly social, constrained budget, tech-dependent.
3. **The Small Business Owner:** Connected to local supply chains, community pillar.
